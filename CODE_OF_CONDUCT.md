# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Roster to be a welcoming project for people who use, build, review, and maintain it. We commit to constructive discussion, respectful disagreement, and collaboration free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarification when needed.
- Focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and code.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's credentials, private repositories, or personal information.
- Help make discussions accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, or publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or limit participation when this code is not followed. Report concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) until a dedicated community contact is published.
